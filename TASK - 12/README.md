## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** created using Tableau Public. The dashboard analyzes sales performance based on **time, category, region, and product performance**.

The main objective is to identify important sales trends, understand category and regional performance, and provide useful business recommendations.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains e-commerce sales information with the following fields:

- Order Date
- Product Name
- Category
- Region
- Quantity
- Sales
- Profit

The dataset contains **3,500 records** covering the period from **2022 to 2024**.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 1. Sales Trend Over Time
A line chart showing how sales vary across different months.

### 2. Sales by Category
A bar chart comparing sales performance across different product categories.

### 3. Sales by Region
A pie chart showing the contribution of each region to total sales.

### 4. Product Performance
A bubble chart used to compare product performance based on sales and profit.

## 🔍 Key Findings

1. **Electronics is the highest-performing category**, generating approximately ₹53.26 lakh in sales, followed by Accessories at ₹42.48 lakh. Office has the lowest sales at approximately ₹10.94 lakh.

2. **West is the top-performing region**, with sales of approximately ₹28.44 lakh. East follows with ₹26.75 lakh, South with ₹26.60 lakh, and North has the lowest sales at approximately ₹24.89 lakh.

3. **Sales vary across the months**, with May recording the highest monthly sales at approximately ₹10.35 lakh, while February has the lowest at approximately ₹7.02 lakh. This indicates noticeable monthly variation in sales performance.

---

## 💡 Suggestions / Recommendations

1. The business should focus more marketing and promotional activities on the **Electronics category**, as it generates the highest sales, while also developing strategies to improve the performance of the Office category.

2. The business should focus on improving sales in the **North region** through targeted promotions, customer-focused offers, and region-specific marketing strategies.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The analysis shows that the e-commerce business performs strongly in the **Electronics category and West region**. However, sales vary across months, while the Office category and North region provide opportunities for improvement.

By strengthening high-performing categories and regions while addressing weaker areas, the business can improve its overall sales performance and make better data-driven decisions.
