# Week 6 – Business Trend Analysis using Area Chart in Tableau

## Objective

Analyze business performance over time using an Area Chart in Tableau and identify important trends and changes that can support business decision-making.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains business-related information such as Order Date, Sales, Category, Sub-Category, Region, and Quantity.

## Fields Used

* **Time-based field:** Order Date
* **Business measure:** Sales

## Visualizations Created

1. **Monthly Sales Trend** – Area Chart
2. **Sales by Category** – Bar Chart
3. **Sales by Region** – Pie Chart
4. **Category Sales and Sub-Category** – Treemap

## Key Business Insights

1. Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, with February recording the lowest sales.
2. Sales increase significantly during the later months, particularly from September to December.
3. November records the highest monthly sales, showing the strongest business performance.
4. Technology is the highest-selling product category.
5. The West region generates the highest sales among the four regions.

## Conclusion

The Tableau analysis provides a clear understanding of business sales performance over time. The Area Chart highlights monthly sales fluctuations and shows stronger performance toward the end of the year. Category and regional analysis identifies Technology and the West region as strong contributors to sales. These insights can help the business focus on high-performing products and regions while developing strategies to improve weaker periods.
