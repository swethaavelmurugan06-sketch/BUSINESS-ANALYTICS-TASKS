# Customer Business Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using the Supermarket Sales dataset in Tableau Public.

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle
- Dataset: Supermarket Sales

## Tools Used
- Tableau Public
- GitHub

## Dashboard Visualizations
- Sales by Customer Type
- Sales by City
- Preferred Payment Method
- Sales by Gender
- Product Category by Customer Type

## Business Questions Answered

1. Which customer segment generates the highest sales?
Member customers generate higher sales than Normal customers.

2. Which payment method is most preferred?
Cash is the most preferred payment method.

3. Which city contributes the highest revenue?
Naypyitaw contributes the highest sales.

4. Which customer group purchases more?
Female customers contribute higher sales than Male customers.

5. Which product category is popular among customer groups?
Food and Beverages and Sports & Travel are among the top-performing categories.

## Business Insights

1. Member customers generate the highest sales revenue.
2. Naypyitaw is the best-performing city.
3. Female customers contribute more revenue than male customers.
4. Cash is the most preferred payment method.
5. Food & Beverages and Sports & Travel perform strongly among customer types.

## Business Recommendations

1. Increase loyalty offers for Member customers.
2. Launch targeted marketing campaigns in Naypyitaw.
3. Ensure sufficient inventory for high-performing product categories.
