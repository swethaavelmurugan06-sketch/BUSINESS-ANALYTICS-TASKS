# Task 10 – Business Performance Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Tableau Public Dashboard

🔗 [View Interactive Dashboard on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/swetha.v1825/viz/task10_17900096608500/Dashboard1?publish=yes)

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze business performance across different categories using a Heat Map and other visualizations in Tableau Public.

## 📌 Dataset

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains business-related information such as Category, Region, Segment, Sales, Order Date, and State.

## 📊 Visualizations Created

1. **Sales Heat Map**
   - Shows Sales across different Categories and Regions.
   - Color intensity represents the Sales value.

2. **Sales by Category**
   - Bar chart showing total Sales for each Category.

3. **Sales Trend Over Time**
   - Line chart showing changes in Sales over time.

4. **Sales by Segment**
   - Pie chart showing Sales distribution across different Segments.

5. **Sales by State**
   - Map visualization showing Sales performance across different states.

## 🔥 Heat Map Analysis

- The Heat Map compares business performance across different Categories and Regions.
- Darker color areas represent higher Sales values.
- Lighter color areas represent lower Sales values.
- The Heat Map helps identify strong and weak Category–Region combinations.
- Sales performance varies across regions and categories, showing that demand is not evenly distributed.

## 💡 Business Insights

1. **Technology** contributes strongly to overall sales compared with the other major product categories.

2. **Consumer** is the largest sales segment, followed by Corporate and Home Office.

3. Sales performance varies across regions, indicating differences in regional customer demand.

4. The Heat Map shows that some Category–Region combinations perform better than others, highlighting areas of strong business performance.

5. The Sales Trend visualization shows changes in business performance over time and helps identify periods of higher and lower sales.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

1. Focus inventory, marketing, and promotional activities on the high-performing Category–Region combinations to maintain strong sales.

2. Analyze low-performing categories and regions further and use targeted promotions, improved product availability, and customer-focused strategies to increase sales.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filter

An interactive filter is included in the dashboard to allow users to explore the data based on different categories/regions.

## ✅ Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance using a Heat Map, bar chart, line chart, pie chart, and map. The analysis helps identify differences in sales performance across categories, regions, segments, and states and supports data-driven business decisions.
