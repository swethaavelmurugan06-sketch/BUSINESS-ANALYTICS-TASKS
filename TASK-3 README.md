## Business Insights
1. The average restaurant rating varies across different cuisines.
2. A few cuisines account for the majority of restaurants, while others have much fewer.
3. Popular cuisines dominate the restaurant distribution.
4. The treemap clearly highlights which cuisines have the largest presence.
5. Understanding cuisine popularity and ratings can help businesses identify customer preferences and market opportunities.
