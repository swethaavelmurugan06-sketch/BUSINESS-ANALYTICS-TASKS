## 🎯 Objective

To analyze and compare different business categories using a Bubble Chart and other visualizations in Tableau Public, and identify the most and least significant business categories.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

The objective of this task is to analyze business performance using categorical and numerical data such as Category, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Order Date, and Region.

A Bubble Chart is used as the main visualization to compare sales performance across different business categories.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* Tableau Public
* Superstore Dataset
* Microsoft Excel

---

## 📊 Visualizations Created

### 1. 🫧 Bubble Chart – Sales by Category

* Compares Sales across different business categories.
* Bubble size represents Sales.
* Different colors represent different categories.
* Helps identify the highest and lowest sales categories.

### 2. 📊 Profit by Category

* A bar chart is used to compare Profit across categories.
* Helps identify which categories contribute more to overall profitability.

### 3. 🥧 Sales by Region

* A pie chart represents Sales distribution across different regions.
* Helps compare regional sales performance.

### 4. 📈 Sales Trend

* A line chart shows how Sales change over time.
* Helps identify periods of higher and lower sales.

### 5. 🔽 Region Filter

* An interactive Region filter is added to the dashboard.
* Users can select different regions and analyze the corresponding business performance.

---

## 💡 5 Business Insights

1. **Technology** is one of the strongest-performing categories based on sales, indicating high customer demand.

2. **Office Supplies** contributes a significant portion of total sales and remains an important business category.

3. **Furniture** contributes to overall sales, but its performance should also be evaluated based on profitability.

4. Sales performance varies across different **regions**, showing that regional factors influence business performance.

5. The **Sales Trend** visualization shows changes in sales over time and helps identify periods of higher and lower business activity.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

1. **Focus on high-performing categories and regions** by maintaining product availability and using targeted marketing strategies to increase sales further.

2. **Improve lower-performing categories** by reviewing pricing, promotions, product selection, and customer demand to increase their sales and profitability.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

The Bubble Chart provides a quick visual comparison of business categories based on sales. Categories with larger bubbles have higher sales, while smaller bubbles indicate comparatively lower sales.

Combining the Bubble Chart with Profit, Regional Sales, and Sales Trend visualizations provides a better overall understanding of business performance.
