# Week 7 – Business Trend Analysis using Scatter Plot in Tableau

## Objective

Analyze business performance using a Scatter Plot in Tableau and identify important trends and changes that can support business decision-making.

## Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

## Visualizations Created

1. Scatter Plot – Sales vs Profit
2. Bar Chart – Sales by Category
3. Line Chart – Sales over Time
4. Pie/Donut Chart – Sales Distribution by Category

## Tableau Dashboard

[View Tableau Public Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/swetha.v1825/viz/Task7_17883662361440/SalesPerformanceAnalysisDashboard?publish=yes)

## Key Insights

* The Scatter Plot shows the relationship between Sales and Profit.
* Higher Sales generally correspond to higher Profit, but some products generate high sales with relatively low profit.
* Some points represent loss-making products where Profit is negative.
* The Category comparison helps identify stronger and weaker business segments.
* The time-based analysis shows changes in business performance over time.

## Conclusion

The Tableau analysis helps identify the relationship between Sales and Profit and highlights profitable, low-profit, and loss-making business areas. These insights can support better pricing, product selection, and business decision-making.
