## E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

### Tableau Public Dashboard

[View Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/swetha.v1825/viz/Ecommerce_Sales_Profit_Analysis/Dashboard1?publish=yes)

### Business Questions

1. How is sales distributed across different product categories?
2. Which product category contributes the most profit?
3. Which category and region combination performs better in terms of profit?
4. How are profit values distributed across e-commerce orders?

### Visualizations

- Box Plot – Sales Distribution by Category
- Packed Bubble Chart – Profit by Category
- Heat Map – Regional Profit
- Histogram – Profit Distribution

### Business Insights

1. Electronics is one of the strongest profit-generating categories.
2. Office has the lowest overall profit contribution.
3. Sales show considerable variation across categories, with high-value outliers.
4. Electronics shows strong profit performance across different regions.
5. Profit distribution is right-skewed, with fewer orders generating much higher profits.

### Conclusion

The dashboard analyzes e-commerce sales and profitability across categories and regions. The analysis helps identify profitable categories, regional performance, sales variation, and profit distribution.
