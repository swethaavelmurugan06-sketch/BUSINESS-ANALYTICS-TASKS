# Week 8 – Business Category Analysis using Tree Map in Tableau

## Objective

Analyze the contribution of different business categories using a Tree Map in Tableau and identify the categories and sub-categories that have the greatest impact on business performance.

## Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

## Visualizations Created

1. Tree Map – Sales Contribution by Category & Sub-Category
2. Scatter Plot – Sales vs Customer Count by Sub-Category
3. Packed Bubble Chart – Sub-Category Sales Performance
4. Stacked Bar Chart – Regional Sales Contribution by Category

## Dashboard

The dashboard combines all four visualizations to provide an interactive view of sales performance across categories, sub-categories, customers, and regions.

## Business Insights

1. Technology sub-categories such as Phones and Machines contribute significantly to overall sales.
2. Furniture sub-categories such as Chairs and Tables represent important contributors to sales.
3. Sales and customer count vary considerably across different sub-categories.
4. The West and East regions show strong overall sales performance compared with the other regions.
5. Sales contribution differs across categories and regions, indicating opportunities for targeted regional strategies.

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing sub-categories and maintain sufficient inventory to support continued sales.
2. Develop region-specific strategies for lower-performing categories to improve their sales contribution.
