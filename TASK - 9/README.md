## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze business trends over time using Tableau Public. The dashboard focuses on sales performance and provides different visualizations to identify trends, comparisons, and patterns.

## 📌 Visualizations Created

1. **Sales Trend Over Time – Line Graph**
   - Shows how sales change over time.
   - Helps identify increasing and decreasing trends, peaks, and drops.

2. **Sales by Category – Bar Chart**
   - Compares sales across different product categories.
   - Helps identify categories with higher and lower sales.

3. **Sales by Region – Pie Chart**
   - Shows the distribution of sales across different regions.
   - Helps compare the contribution of each region.

4. **Sales by Segment Over Time – Area Chart**
   - Shows sales trends for different customer segments over time.
   - Helps understand changes in segment-wise sales performance.

## 🔍 Interactive Filter

- **Region Filter**
- Allows users to select a specific region and interactively analyze the dashboard.

## 💡 Business Insights

1. Sales show changes over time, with certain periods recording higher sales than others.
2. The line graph helps identify the highest and lowest sales periods.
3. Sales performance varies across different product categories.
4. The West region contributes a significant portion of total sales, while the South region has comparatively lower sales.
5. Customer segments show different sales patterns over time.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing categories and regions to maintain and improve sales performance.
2. Analyze low-performing periods and regions to identify opportunities for improving sales and customer engagement.
